Dr Andrew K. Martin has published research across social cognition, cognitive neuroscience, neuropsychology, psychosis, ageing, non-invasive brain stimulation, and related areas.

His work includes experimental, neuroscientific, and clinical research examining the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying social behaviour, perspective-taking, sense of agency, memory, and social cognition across healthy and clinical populations.
