## Principal Investigator

### Dr Andrew K. Martin

Dr Martin's research focuses on social cognition, perspective-taking, theory of mind, executive functions, psychosis, ageing, loneliness, and the neural mechanisms that support human social behaviour.

He has published extensively in leading journals including The Journal of Neuroscience, Cerebral Cortex, Neuropsychologia, Schizophrenia Research, Psychological Medicine, and Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience.
